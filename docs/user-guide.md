# User and Administrator Guide

## Finance staff

- Sign in at the finance dashboard.
- Upload receipt images from the Upload Receipt section.
- Review low-confidence matches in the Review Queue.
- Search invoices and payments to verify reconciliation status.
- Review audit logs for operational visibility.

## Administrators

- Manage users and roles through the admin control surface.
- Configure deployment secrets and environment variables.
- Review monitoring dashboards and incident alerts.
- Perform backup and restore operations as part of disaster recovery.

## Support and troubleshooting

- Check the health endpoint at `/health`.
- Review logs from the backend and OCR engine containers.
- Verify database connectivity and backup availability during incidents.
